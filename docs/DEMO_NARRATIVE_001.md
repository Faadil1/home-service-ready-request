## Evaluator story

The film proves one narrow product promise: a homeowner who missed contact can turn service context into a structured service-ready request without the interface pretending an appointment has been booked.

The evidence must preserve the product truth boundaries already validated at Gates 6 and 6.5:
- request received is not booking confirmation;
- Access Window is a preference, not provider inventory;
- the product does not diagnose, quote, dispatch emergency service, assign a technician, or guarantee response time;
- immediate hazards leave the ordinary request flow;
- the service-ready brief makes unresolved items visible.
